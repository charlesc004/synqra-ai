# Northstar Digital Operations — IT Security Policy
Document: NDO-SEC-001. Version 1.0. Effective 1 January 2026.

## Access control
Every user must have an individual account. Shared accounts are prohibited. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for production access and administrator accounts. The IT team reviews access privileges every 90 days. Managers approve access requests and the IT team records the approval before provisioning.

## Account lifecycle
Disable accounts within four hours of a confirmed departure. Privileged access requires a business justification and manager approval. Emergency access lasts no more than 24 hours and must be reviewed on the next business day.

## Data handling
Classify documents as public, internal, or restricted. Restricted material may be shared only with named authorized recipients. Company data must remain on approved services. Never include access tokens or passwords in support tickets, documents, or logs.

## Version notice
This original policy is retained for comparison. NDO-SEC-001 version 2.0 replaces the access-review interval, MFA scope, and departure deadline from 1 July 2026. Consult the revised policy for current requirements. Report suspected compromise immediately; incident response is defined in NDO-IR-001.
