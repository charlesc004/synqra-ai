# Northstar Digital Operations — Employee Handbook
Document: NDO-HR-001. Version 1.0. Effective 1 January 2026.

## Working together
Northstar is a fictional technology company with engineering, operations, and customer support teams. Its working week is Monday through Friday. Shared collaboration hours are 10:00–15:00 UTC, with flexible schedules outside those hours. Managers approve exceptions in writing.

## Leave and onboarding
Full-time employees receive 25 working days of annual leave, in addition to local public holidays. Request planned leave at least ten working days ahead. Urgent medical leave should be reported to the manager as soon as practical. The People Operations team, not the IT team, owns leave decisions.

New employees complete security training within five working days. Their manager assigns a buddy, records their role, and requests the minimum required system access. Access is never granted solely from an informal chat request. Remote-work eligibility is described in NDO-HR-002.

## Help and escalation
For employment questions, contact the fictional People Operations inbox people@northstar.example. Report suspected misconduct to a manager or People Operations. Customer incidents follow the Incident Response Playbook; leave questions do not use the incident channel.
