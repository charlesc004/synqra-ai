# Northstar Digital Operations — IT Security Policy, Revised
Document: NDO-SEC-001. Version 2.0. Effective 1 July 2026. Supersedes version 1.0.

## Access control changes
Every user must have an individual account. Shared accounts remain prohibited. Multi-factor authentication is now mandatory for all company systems, not only production and administrator accounts. The IT team must review access privileges every 30 days, replacing the previous 90-day interval.

## Account lifecycle changes
Disable accounts within one hour of a confirmed departure, replacing the earlier four-hour deadline. Privileged access still requires a business justification and manager approval. Emergency access expires after eight hours, replacing 24 hours, and must be reviewed on the next business day.

## Unchanged requirements
Managers approve access before provisioning. Restricted material is shared only with named authorized recipients. Company data stays on approved services. Never record passwords or tokens in support tickets, documents, or logs.

## Implementation obligations
IT must complete an access review by 31 July 2026 and retain the signed review record for 12 months. Security owns policy interpretation. Compare this document with version 1.0 when explaining changes; do not merge superseded time limits into current guidance.
