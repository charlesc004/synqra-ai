# Northstar Digital Operations — Remote Work Policy
Document: NDO-HR-002. Version 1.0. Effective 1 February 2026.

## Eligibility
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week after completing onboarding and obtaining manager approval. Fully remote arrangements require a written agreement with People Operations. This policy does not change local employment terms.

## Availability
Remote employees remain available during the collaboration window, 10:00–15:00 UTC. Keep the team calendar current and post a handover note before planned absences. Managers can approve alternate hours for time-zone needs.

## Secure working environment
Use a company-managed device, disk encryption, multi-factor authentication, and the approved VPN when accessing internal systems. Do not copy company documents into personal storage or use public shared computers. Lock the screen whenever unattended. Report lost equipment within one hour using the Incident Response Playbook.

## Expenses and review
An approved home-office purchase may be reimbursed up to USD 300 once per calendar year. Finance requires an itemized receipt and manager approval before purchase. Managers review remote arrangements quarterly. The IT Security Policy governs access controls; it does not set the remote-work day allowance.
