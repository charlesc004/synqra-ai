# Northstar Digital Operations — Project Delivery Guidelines
Document: NDO-DEL-001. Version 1.0.

## Gate 1: Discovery
The delivery lead records scope, stakeholders, risks, and measurable acceptance criteria before work starts. Assign a named owner to each risk. The customer sponsor signs the scope record. Unresolved critical risks block implementation.

## Gate 2: Implementation review
The engineering lead prepares a test summary, security review, and rollback plan. Hold the review at least five working days before the planned launch. Track outstanding defects with an owner and target date. A Severity 1 defect blocks launch.

## Gate 3: Acceptance and launch
The delivery lead and customer sponsor confirm acceptance criteria in writing. The engineering lead verifies that monitoring, support handover, and rollback steps work. Record the launch decision in the project workspace.

## Required follow-up actions
The delivery lead schedules a lessons-learned meeting within ten working days after launch. The support lead verifies the customer handover checklist before the first support shift. The engineering lead reviews the monitoring dashboard daily for the first five working days. These are obligations to review; extracting them with AI does not create or assign authoritative tasks automatically.
