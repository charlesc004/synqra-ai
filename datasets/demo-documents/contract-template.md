# Northstar Digital Operations — Fictional Services Contract Template
Document: NDO-CON-001. Draft template for demonstration only.

## Parties and scope
This template describes a fictional services arrangement between Northstar Digital Operations and a customer to be named. The project statement must define deliverables, acceptance criteria, schedule, and fees. Missing party names, dates, and prices are intentional placeholders; do not infer them from other documents.

## Acceptance
The customer has ten working days after delivery to review each deliverable against written acceptance criteria. Material defects must be documented. Silence is not treated as acceptance in this template. Changes to scope require a written change record signed by both parties.

## Confidentiality and data
Each party protects the other party's confidential information and limits access to personnel who need it for the engagement. Security obligations must be agreed in the project statement. This template does not grant permission to disclose private documents.

## Review
Commercial and legal reviewers must complete the template before use. No governing jurisdiction, liability cap, payment amount, or signatory is specified. Automated comparisons are drafting assistance and cannot establish a definitive contractual interpretation.
